Privacy Policy

History Uploader · Effective 18 October 2026

History Uploader is a private tool that one channel owner runs on their own computer to publish their own videos to their own YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public. This policy explains what it accesses through Google and YouTube, and what happens to that data.

YouTube API Services

History Uploader uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's own use of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What it accesses

When the owner signs in with Google, History Uploader asks permission to:

  • Upload and manage videos on the owner's channel: titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails, captions, playlists, privacy status and scheduled publish times.
  • Post comments from the channel on the channel's own videos.
  • Read statistics for the channel's own videos: views, likes, comments, shares, subscribers gained, average view duration and average percentage viewed.
  • Read basic channel information (channel name and subscriber count) to confirm it is connected to the right channel.

It does not access anyone else's account, private messages, contacts, email, payment details or watch history.

How the data is used

  • To publish and schedule the owner's videos and post the owner's comments.
  • To learn which of the channel's own videos perform best, so the tool can choose better topics and write better titles and openings for future videos. For this, the titles, opening lines and performance numbers of the channel's own videos may be included in instructions sent to the AI writing services the owner uses to draft scripts (OpenAI and Anthropic). No sign-in tokens are ever sent to them.

The data is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not shared with anyone else.

Where it is stored

  • The Google sign-in token is saved in a file on the owner's own computer that only the owner's user account can read.
  • Video statistics are saved in a local database on the same computer.
  • History Uploader has no server, website accounts or cloud database of its own.

Keeping and deleting data

Data stays on the owner's computer until the owner deletes it. Deleting the tool's data folder and token file removes it all. Access can be withdrawn at any time from your Google Account's third-party connections page (also reachable from Google security settings), after which History Uploader can no longer reach the channel.

Google API Services User Data Policy

History Uploader's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Children

History Uploader is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from them.
