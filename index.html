History Uploader

A private tool that publishes one creator's own history videos to their own YouTube channel.

What it does

History Uploader runs on its owner's computer. It takes videos the owner has produced (fact-checked history Shorts and long-form episodes) and uses the YouTube API to:

  • upload them to the owner's channel with a title, description, tags, thumbnail and captions,
  • schedule when each video goes public and add it to a playlist,
  • post one comment from the channel on each of its own new videos, and
  • read the channel's own view and audience-retention statistics, so future videos can be improved.

Who can use it

Only the channel owner who runs it. History Uploader is not offered to the public, has no sign-up, and does not access any other person's account or channel.

Google and YouTube

History Uploader uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy. Read the History Uploader privacy policy for what this tool accesses and stores.
